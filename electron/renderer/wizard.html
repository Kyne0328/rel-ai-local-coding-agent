Four-step setup

Connect ChatGPT to your local repositories.

Rel.AI runs locally, publishes one secure HTTPS MCP endpoint through your ngrok account, and keeps that ChatGPT app URL stable.

01
Local by defaultYour repositories remain on this computer.
02
Scoped accessChatGPT receives explicit workspace tools instead of unrestricted shell access.
03
Stable connectionThe permanent tunnel URL can be reused after restarts.
Step 2 of 4

Configure the local service.

Choose the local port and keep the generated approval token private. The token protects the dashboard and approves ChatGPT OAuth access.

Approval token

Stored in ~/.rel-ai-mcp/.env. ChatGPT will ask for this value when you approve the app.

Step 3 of 4

Configure the secure connection.

Use your ngrok account key and static domain. Rel.AI stores the key only in its private local configuration.

Connection preview managed ngrok tunnel for <domain> on local port 3333
Step 4 of 4

Review and launch.

Rel.AI will save the configuration, start the local MCP service, prepare the managed ngrok tunnel, and publish the ChatGPT endpoint.

After launch, copy the MCP endpoint into ChatGPT Apps, choose OAuth, and approve with your approval token.
