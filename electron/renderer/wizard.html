What happens next

After the code appears, open ChatGPT on the web. On Plus or Pro, open Plugins from the sidebar or Settings → Plugins, add Rel.AI MCP, and choose Connect. On Business, Enterprise, or Edu, open the Rel.AI app provided under your workspace Apps. When the Rel.AI authorization page asks for a pairing code, enter the code from this screen.

If Rel.AI is unavailable, Developer mode or workspace approval may be required for your plan.
