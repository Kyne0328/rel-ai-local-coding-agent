Connect to ChatGPT ⌄
  1. Wait until the Secure MCP Tunnel shows Connected, then open the main dashboard and go to Connection.
  2. Save the provided Rel.AI icon before opening ChatGPT plugin setup.
  3. Use Rel.AI MCP as the name, choose Tunnel, select this computer's tunnel ID, and choose No authentication.
  4. Click Scan Tools, confirm the Rel.AI tools appear, then click Create.
  5. After creation, open Manage and upload the saved Rel.AI icon as the connector logo.
  6. Keep Rel.AI running while ChatGPT works with your projects.
